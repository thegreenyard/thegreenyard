What Does Abundance Look Like Around the World?

There is no single way to create an abundant landscape. Some people transform a small front yard, while others manage farms, orchards, nurseries, community gardens, or tropical food forests. The Green Yard documents those different approaches while keeping the people and their stories at the center of every tour.
