My Mango Journey

One Tree Never Tells the Whole Story

Mango has become one of the plants I have documented repeatedly in The Green Yard. Different trees, seasons, heat events, winter conditions, flowering cycles, and fruiting attempts have all added another piece to the story.

This page is designed to bring those moments together so the mango journey can be followed over time instead of being scattered across years of individual videos.

This Is a Plant Journey

This page documents what has happened in my Phoenix food forest. It is not meant to be a complete step-by-step mango growing manual. The videos, milestones, and observations below show the real story as it continues to unfold.

What I’ve Observed

Lessons That Keep Showing Up

These should stay high-level and observational. The goal is to share what the journey has revealed without turning the page into the full growing system that could eventually become part of paid education.

Tree Age Matters

Young mango trees and more established trees can respond very differently to the same Phoenix conditions.

Every Season Adds Context

One hot summer or one cold winter is only one chapter. The longer these trees are documented, the more useful the story becomes.

Location Changes the Experience

Trees growing only a short distance apart can experience noticeably different conditions within the same food forest.
