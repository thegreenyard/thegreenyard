More Than Growing Guides

The ongoing story of a Phoenix food forest

Growing fruit in Phoenix is not a simple list of instructions. Every tree responds differently to extreme heat, dry air, irrigation, soil conditions, and winter cold.

Over the years, I have documented our trees from the day they were planted through yearly updates, freeze damage, recovery, first flowers, first fruit, harvesting, pruning, and everything we learned along the way.

This library brings those videos, Shorts, photographs, and lessons together by plant. Instead of seeing one isolated update, you can follow the complete journey of each tree as it grows and changes over time.

The older videos are not separate from The Green Yard. They are the beginning of the story that brought us here.
