Years of Experiments, Documented in One Place

The Green Yard did not appear overnight. It has been built plant by plant, season by season, with plenty of successes, mistakes, surprises, and changes along the way.

This section of the site brings that history together so the plants can be followed over years instead of being reduced to a single planting video or one seasonal update.
